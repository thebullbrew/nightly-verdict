The Method

Compare strategies on net, never gross. The nightly side gets charged everything it truly consumes: platform and management cuts on the full gross (cleaning fees included), the cleaner's real invoice, utilities guests have no reason to spare, supplies, the insurance uplift, and a monthly furnishing reserve. The long-term side gets charged vacancy, management, and maintenance — honestly, but without inventing costs it does not have. Shared fixed costs are applied to both, so the gap you see is the strategy gap, not the house gap.

Then the only two questions that matter: at what occupancy does nightly merely tie the tenant, and how many turnovers a month are you buying for the difference? If the premium does not pay for the work, take the tenant and go find another house. That is the whole game.
